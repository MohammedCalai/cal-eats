# Customers, loyalty and restaurant-funded promotions

The loyalty dashboard derives all figures from completed, non-fully-refunded orders belonging to the selected restaurant and never includes future-dated orders. The reporting timestamp and configurable definitions are returned with every result. New means exactly one qualifying order; returning means more than one; regular counts qualifying orders inside the configured rolling window; lapsed means the last qualifying order is older than the configured threshold. Frequency is the rolling-window count normalised to 30 days. These labels intentionally overlap. Current/prior trends use equal elapsed month intervals and report both order volume and repeat-customer rate.

Growth suggestions are deterministic rules and never create or fund a campaign. Campaign activation always requires an owner request, and snapshots the segment definitions, thresholds, reporting time and matched/consented counts used. Promotion delivery uses strict, recorded opt-in per channel. In-app and push choices default off; legacy merchant-offer opt-in migrates only to in-app and its historical consent time remains unknown rather than being invented. Operational order notifications do not depend on marketing consent. Push is a preference only until a provider is separately implemented and verified.

Supported offers are a free item, percentage discount, or fixed discount. They are restaurant-funded and exclusive: combining offers is rejected. Platform-funded offers and public first-order acquisition are rejected until separately authorised. Reservation is atomic with checkout, bounded by campaign budget, released if the order is rejected/expired, and redeemed on completion.

`subtotal_minor` is the discounted food amount actually charged. `gross_food_minor`, `restaurant_discount_minor`, and the per-line allocation in `promotion_snapshot` reconcile the basket. Free items remain zero-price. The existing versioned 7% policy is unchanged: commission applies to discounted paid food, plus delivery only when the order's immutable policy snapshot says `food_plus_delivery`. Refunds are capped at net paid amounts and reverse commission using that original snapshot.

`POST /api/orders/quote` accepts restaurant, basket, fulfillment/address and optional offer grant fields. It repeats server-owned menu/modifier, delivery, consent, promotion budget/limit and commission validation without creating an order or reserving a grant. Its `sideEffects: false` response supplies gross food, restaurant discount, net food, delivery, total, commission and allocation details for customer clients; checkout remains authoritative.
