# Cal-Eats merchant commercial schedule — UNAPPROVED DRAFT

**Not published, not active and not available for merchant acceptance.**

This working draft records only the commercial arrangements already selected for Cal-Eats. It is not a complete merchant agreement. No document version, approval date or effective date has been assigned. Those details and the final agreement must be reviewed before activation.

The proposed content, menu and photo section below is **new, unapproved wording for review**. It does not record an existing signed grant from any restaurant.

## Parties and service

Cal-Eats is the marketplace operated by CALAI LTD, company number 16995419, registered office 62 Cades Close, Luton, LU1 5QQ, England. The restaurant supplies the customer's food and provides the delivery or collection service offered for its orders.

## Commission

The normal Cal-Eats commission is **7% of the order value after restaurant-funded offers and discounts, including the delivery fee actually charged**.

For example, £15 of food less a £4 restaurant offer, plus a £2 delivery fee, gives a commission basis of £13. At 7%, the commission is £0.91 and restaurant earnings before any later recorded adjustments are £12.09.

The saved order breakdown shows the applicable rate, discounts, delivery charge, commission and restaurant earnings. An existing order keeps its saved commission rate and breakdown.

## Commission-free periods

Cal-Eats may schedule commission-free periods for an individual restaurant. A period's start and end are recorded for that restaurant. New orders placed during that period use the scheduled commission rate. Previously placed orders retain their saved rate.

## Contract period and fees

The restaurant contract lasts **24 months from the owner's recorded acceptance**, with its normal 7% commission fixed for that period. Scheduled commission-free periods are temporary waivers of that normal commission. They do not extend the contract period.

Cal-Eats charges no additional setup, monthly subscription, administration or per-order platform fee under this arrangement. No customer platform service fee applies for the initial release. The restaurant uses its own delivery drivers; Cal-Eats does not supply delivery drivers.

A later default agreement or change of restaurant owner login does not automatically restart an existing contract period. A new contract period requires a reviewed renewal agreement and a new affirmative owner acceptance.

## Card processing and payment flow

Cal-Eats bears normal card-processing costs. They are not an additional deduction from the restaurant's earnings under the commission arrangement above, including during a commission-free period.

The customer authorises their card at checkout. Payment is captured when the restaurant confirms the order. Completed orders become eligible for transfer to the restaurant's connected payment account. The saved payment and transfer status is shown separately from the bank payout status.

The restaurant supplies its genuine business, verification and bank details through the payment provider's secure setup. Completing bank setup alone does not activate customer payments.

## Bank payouts

The planned payout schedule is weekly on Monday. Payouts depend on completed business and bank verification, available funds, the payment provider's processing and the receiving bank. A scheduled payout date or estimated arrival is not a promise that money has already reached the restaurant's bank.

## Proposed merchant content, menu and photo permission — UNAPPROVED

### Authority and ownership

The business remains the owner of its business name, branding, menu text and images, or holds the necessary permission from their owner. Before supplying content, its authorised representative must be entitled to permit the uses described here, including any required photographer or other third-party permission. Content being publicly available elsewhere does not by itself establish that permission. The business must tell Cal-Eats about required credits, expiry dates or other restrictions before the content is used. Cal-Eats must honour those restrictions or decline to use that content.

### Limited permission to operate and present the service

The business gives CALAI LTD non-exclusive permission, at no separate content-licence fee, to reproduce and display its supplied business name, branding, menu information and images for its Cal-Eats listing, customer ordering and related business/support operations. This includes Cal-Eats websites and apps, the business and administrator platforms, and necessary order information and receipts. It also includes truthful official Cal-Eats App Store and Google Play listing materials that show the business's listing, menu or supplied images to explain or promote the Cal-Eats service.

Permission is limited to those purposes and does not transfer ownership. Use for unrelated products, separate content sales or other purposes requires separate permission.

Cal-Eats may resize, crop, compress and format content as needed for those surfaces, while preserving its meaning and avoiding a misleading representation of the food or business. Cal-Eats may permit its hosting, image-processing and content-delivery providers, and the app stores hosting its official listing materials, to handle the content only as needed to provide those permitted uses. This does not give those providers a separate right to exploit the content for unrelated purposes.

### Menu, image and food-information responsibility

The business remains responsible for supplying and keeping accurate its business information, dishes, descriptions, prices, availability and required food/allergen information. It must provide the legally required allergen information before purchase and with delivery or collection as applicable, and keep its preparation and fulfilment arrangements consistent with that information. A technical format check or publication on Cal-Eats does not establish that Cal-Eats has verified an ingredient, allergen statement or photograph.

Images must fairly represent the relevant dish or business. An illustrative image must be clearly identified as illustrative. The business must promptly correct out-of-date information and notify Cal-Eats where a safety issue, inaccurate publication or rights restriction needs urgent attention. Cal-Eats should confirm factual menu corrections with the business; it may hide disputed or potentially unsafe content while the issue is investigated.

### Corrections, complaints and removal

The business must cooperate with reasonable requests to correct content or establish permission to use it. Cal-Eats may restrict or remove content subject to a credible rights, accuracy or safety concern, contact the business about the issue and consider a corrected or properly authorised replacement. The existing Cal-Eats support/reporting routes can be used to raise such concerns. Publication is not a guarantee that every upload has undergone manual or automated review.

### When the listing closes

When the business's Cal-Eats listing closes, Cal-Eats will stop new promotional use of its content and remove it from active public surfaces it controls as soon as reasonably practicable. It will promptly update any affected official store-listing materials; app-store review, caches and copies already downloaded may be outside its immediate control. Content needed to fulfil an existing order may remain available for that limited purpose.

Only necessary historical order/transaction records, complaint or rights evidence, and protected backup copies may be retained and used for those limited purposes under applicable law and the existing approved retention arrangements. This paragraph does not extend a retention period, change the Privacy Notice or permit continued public promotion after closure.
