## Review-notes text to finalise after those checks

Cal-Eats is a UK food marketplace operated by CALAI LTD. Restaurants supply the food and their delivery or collection service. This submission is the customer app; the separate restaurant tablet platform and platform administration are not the customer sign-in role.

Use the dedicated customer credentials entered in the private review-access fields. The account is already email-verified, so its inbox is not required. New customers receive a welcome email with a verification link and can browse or contact support while verification is pending; verification is required to order.

Browse restaurants and menus without allowing location access. You may enter a UK postcode manually; “Use my location” is optional foreground reverse geocoding, not background tracking. The final review-access fields must specify the available restaurant, its service postcode, fulfilment modes and operating hours. Do not assume all restaurants deliver.

Add menu items, choose any required options, open Basket and proceed to checkout. The app calculates eligible offers and the order total. Food is a physical purchase; online payment uses the external hosted payment provider rather than Apple or Google digital-content billing. Final ordering instructions must be verified against the enabled production service before these notes are submitted.

Orders shows current order status and ETA, and completed-order history within its supported period. An order can show restaurant messages and help options. Messages provides the customer's restaurant and Cal-Eats support conversations. Customer ratings are quick shop-star ratings without public comments.

When signed in, customers can report a restaurant/menu or an individual merchant message and block merchant contact from the native restaurant/chat controls. Android 22 guests receive an explicit sign-in action for those safety controls; current iOS 17 does not contain that correction and needs a new checked binary before that guest behavior is described as available on both platforms. Reports go to Cal-Eats platform support. Blocking merchant messages/offers does not suppress necessary order-status updates or Cal-Eats support. Report/block is not a claim that all images or every language are automatically filtered.

Offers contains merchant campaigns and any Cal-Eats voucher granted to this account. Targeted in-app and push marketing use separate account preferences. Notification permission is optional; order and message progress can still be read in the app. Customer alerts are silent. Android 21 and 22 include the quiet-notification explanation/setup prompt, but the recorded Samsung physical-phone prompt/banner confirmation was for build 21; phone settings still control lock-screen/pop-up display. Describe iOS17 according to its own verified controls. iPhone Live Activities are an implemented live-order feature; do not claim physical-device completion/termination coverage beyond the recorded evidence.

Account provides saved addresses, account details, marketing/notification preferences and help. To initiate actual account and eligible-personal-data deletion, open Account → Security & privacy → Delete your account and confirm the password/intention. Staff review the verified request, remove eligible personal information and login access, and confirm completion. Necessary financial/legal records remain for the explained purposes; this is not merely account disabling. Without signing in or reinstalling, a customer can use the public deletion resource at https://eats.calai.info/account-deletion/.

The immutable approved Privacy Notice and Terms are available at https://eats.calai.info/legal/privacy/2026-11-08-v1 and https://eats.calai.info/legal/terms/2026-11-08-v1. Intentional allergy-note entry is currently disabled; the app is not a medical or fitness service.
