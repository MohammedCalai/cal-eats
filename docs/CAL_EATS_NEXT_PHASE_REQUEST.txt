## 9. Execute approved refunds and update financial records

After an authorised admin approves a complaint refund, execute the corresponding financial operations automatically.

For card and Apple Pay payments:

- Refund against the original payment through the provider.
- Support full and partial amounts.
- Prevent cumulative refunds exceeding the remaining refundable amount.
- Adjust restaurant earnings and commission according to the documented policy.
- Perform necessary transfer reversals or settlement adjustments.
- Update customer, restaurant and admin views from confirmed results.

Do not assume that refunding the customer automatically reverses the restaurant’s transfer or returns Cal-Eats’ commission. Implement those operations explicitly.

Handle funds that are pending, already transferred or already paid to the restaurant’s bank.

If funds have already been paid out, record the correct recovery or subsequent settlement adjustment. Do not assume a completed bank payout can always be reversed.

Any hold on disputed earnings must follow the agreed restaurant terms and use a supported provider mechanism. Do not freeze unrelated earnings because one order is disputed.

A customer’s valid remedy must not depend indefinitely on recovering money from the restaurant first.

### Cash refunds

Record:

- Who must return the money.
- The approved amount.
- The repayment method.
- Completion evidence or confirmation.
- Any required cash-commission adjustment.

Do not attempt a card refund for cash that never passed through the provider.

Prevent the restaurant and Cal-Eats from both refunding the same amount.

### Reliability and reconciliation

Maintain an append-only financial ledger. Correct settled history through reversals and adjustments.

Use durable idempotency keys, unique provider references and concurrency controls for orders, payments, refunds, transfers, payouts and commission collection.

Repeated webhooks, worker restarts and simultaneous admin actions must not create duplicate money movements.

Reconcile the ledger against provider transactions and payout results automatically. Surface mismatches for investigation.
