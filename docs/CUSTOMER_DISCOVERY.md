# Customer discovery — 28 October 2026

Customer browsing now uses compact, image-led cards, a two-row horizontal restaurant selection, personalised-offer and quick-option shelves, and a two-column results view on phones. Search stays visible while the feed scrolls. Cuisine shortcuts, delivery/collection, current offers, estimated-time ordering and owner-set delivery-fee ordering are functional. Each shelf has a complete results view; all 21 demo restaurants remain reachable. Tablet and desktop layouts use the same data with more columns.

The browser customer app and native iPhone/Android customer source share eligibility and collection helpers. Expired, redeemed and future offers are excluded from offer shelves. Cards show actual supplied cuisine, estimated time, fulfilment availability and owner-set delivery prices; they do not fabricate star ratings, review counts or sponsored rankings. Existing illustrative food imagery stays labelled as demo imagery. An available personalised offer selected through a restaurant card is carried into the existing reviewed checkout, where eligibility and prices are revalidated.

The Cal-Eats charcoal/blue theme and approved logo remain in place. Discovery removes long restaurant descriptions from cards and collapses postcode editing so choices appear sooner. Restaurant menus retain their existing food, modifier, allergen and basket controls. A new menu opens at the top rather than inheriting a feed scroll position.

Verification includes complete-restaurant retention, actual delivery/collection time and fee ordering, time-bound offer eligibility, customer TypeScript, iOS/Android JavaScript bundle exports, and phone browser walkthroughs. The walkthrough checked side-scrolling without page overflow, all-results access, text search, cuisine and offer filters, opening a matching restaurant and carrying the offer to checkout. Screenshots are responsive browser views, not physical-device or TestFlight evidence. Store/account and physical-device release gates remain separate.
