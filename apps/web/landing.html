Good to know

Questions before launch

Where will Cal-Eats be available?

The marketplace is preparing to launch in Luton. The local preview uses fictional restaurants and sample LU1/LU2 delivery areas.

Who delivers the food?

Participating restaurants use their own delivery drivers. Collection is also supported. Cal-Eats does not provide a courier fleet.

Can I use the preview now?

Yes. You can explore the local customer journey and sample orders. Payments, restaurant data and order activity in the preview are simulated.

How do restaurants manage their menus and orders?

Cal-Eats has one customer app and one business platform. Restaurants use the business platform on their tablet to upload photos, update items and prices, manage availability and handle orders.
