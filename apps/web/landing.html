Good to know

Questions, answered.

Does Cal-Eats charge a customer service fee?

No. Cal-Eats does not add a platform service fee. Restaurants may charge for delivery, and that charge is shown separately before checkout.

Are menu prices marked up by Cal-Eats?

No. Each restaurant sets its menu prices, and Cal-Eats does not add a markup to them.

Where can I order?

Participating restaurants and their delivery or collection options vary by area. The app shows what is available for your address.

Who delivers the food?

Participating restaurants use their own delivery drivers. Collection is also supported. Cal-Eats does not provide a courier fleet.

How will I get the app?

The iPhone and Android download buttons will connect you to the official stores when Cal-Eats is publicly available. Existing invited users can continue using their invitation.

How do restaurants manage their menus and orders?

Cal-Eats has one customer app and one business platform. Restaurants use the business platform on their tablet to upload photos, update items and prices, manage availability and handle orders.
