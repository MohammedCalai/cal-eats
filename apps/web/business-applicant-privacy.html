For restaurant applicants

Your application. Your information.

This notice explains how we use your personal information when you ask to list your restaurant on Cal-Eats.

Updated 14 November 2026. This application notice is separate from the customer Privacy Notice and does not sign you up to a merchant agreement.

Who is responsible

CALAI LTD, company number 16995419, operates Cal-Eats and is the controller of the personal information covered by this notice. Our registered office is 62 Cades Close, Luton, LU1 5QQ, England.

For application or privacy questions, email support@calai.info.

What we collect

The application asks for your name, email address, business name, full business address and mobile number. We also keep the application reference, submission date, application and access status, acknowledgement-email delivery records, and relevant review correspondence and security records.

We collect these details from you. If you act for a business, please provide contact details you are authorised to share. You do not choose or submit a password on this form. Do not put bank details, card numbers, identity documents or passwords into application correspondence.

The contact fields are needed to identify and review the business and reach you. Applying is voluntary, but we cannot process an application through this form without those details.

How we use your information

  • Record and review your application, identify the restaurant and discuss whether it can join Cal-Eats.
  • Send an acknowledgement and contact you about next steps. Our stated application response target is within 72 hours.
  • Prepare initial business access through an administrator if we proceed. An application creates a private pending record; it does not immediately enable business sign-in or public ordering.
  • Keep necessary decision, access and communication records, prevent duplicate applications and protect the service against misuse.

Where you would personally be a party to the merchant contract, we use information necessary to take steps you request before entering that contract. Where you represent a company or another business, we rely on our legitimate interests in reviewing prospective restaurant partners and communicating with their representatives. Necessary application, security and dispute records also serve our legitimate interests in running and protecting the service. We rely on legal obligation where a specific law requires us to keep or disclose information.

Your right to object: you can object to processing based on legitimate interests by emailing support@calai.info. We will consider your circumstances and explain whether there are overriding lawful grounds to continue.

Acknowledgements and basic checks may be automated, but application approval is reviewed by the Calai team. This flow does not make a solely automated decision with a legal or similarly significant effect.

Who receives it

Authorised Calai staff can review applications and prepare business access. Necessary hosting, database and email-delivery providers process information to operate these services. We may share relevant information with professional advisers or public authorities where necessary for legal obligations or establishing, exercising or defending legal claims.

Your pending restaurant application is not a public listing. If your business joins, business details needed for its public listing and ongoing merchant service are handled during onboarding.

Processing outside the UK

Service providers may process information outside the UK. Any restricted transfer requires a valid UK transfer route, such as UK adequacy regulations or appropriate contractual safeguards, with the required assessment where applicable. Contact support@calai.info for details of the locations and safeguards applicable to your application, including how to obtain information about the relevant safeguard. This notice does not claim that every supplier stores all information only in the UK.

How long we keep it

Business applications do not currently have an automatic 30-day deletion rule. We determine the necessary retention by whether the application is still being reviewed or followed up, whether the business joins, and whether records are needed for security, a complaint, a legal claim or a legal record-keeping duty. Relevant information may become part of the merchant relationship if you join.

You can ask us to withdraw an application or delete information that is no longer needed. We review those requests individually and explain any information we need to retain and why. The customer chat and order-contact retention periods do not set a deletion deadline for business applications.

Your rights and questions

Depending on the circumstances, you can request access to your personal information, correction, deletion, restriction of processing or a portable copy, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. These rights are not absolute. We may need to verify your identity before acting and will explain any lawful limit.

Email support@calai.info with your request and the email or application reference you used. Please do not send passwords or full bank or card details. We normally respond within one calendar month, subject to identity checks and any lawful extension.

You can also raise concerns with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office through ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint. We welcome the opportunity to help resolve your concerns.
