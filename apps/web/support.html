Cal-Eats support

How can we help?

Find help with your order, your account or getting started with Cal-Eats.

Help with an order

Open Orders in the Cal-Eats app, select your order and choose Get order support. Your request goes to the restaurant and replies stay in the same conversation. You can also message the restaurant from an active order.

For help from Cal-Eats, email support@calai.info. Include your order reference and the email address on your account.

Account or app help

Open Account → Help & support in the app to send a request and read replies. If you cannot sign in, email support@calai.info.

To delete your account and eligible personal data, use Account → Security & privacy → Delete your account, or request account deletion without the app. Our team verifies each request and explains any necessary retained records.

Order problems and requests for a remedy

We review order complaints and any request for a refund case by case, with the restaurant where relevant. A refund is not automatic or guaranteed. The appropriate outcome depends on what happened and your legal rights; it may include a replacement, price reduction or refund.

Tell us your order reference and explain the problem. We consider your explanation, the restaurant’s reply and relevant order or payment records, and explain the outcome. Keep any helpful receipts or photos if available. We may ask for reasonable evidence and will explain how to provide it; photos are not required in every case and other relevant records or your explanation can help establish what happened. A missing photo or a short reporting period does not automatically remove your rights. Please do not email full payment-card details.

Where appropriate, we may offer an optional goodwill voucher. You do not have to accept a voucher instead of a money refund or another remedy to which you are legally entitled. Nothing here limits your statutory rights.

Start with Orders → your order → Get order support in the app, or email support@calai.info if you need Cal-Eats to help review the issue. See the Customer Terms for the full order and complaints provisions.
