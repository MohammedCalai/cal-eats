Cal-Eats support

How can we help?

Find help with your order, your account or getting started with Cal-Eats.

Help with an order

Open Orders in the Cal-Eats app, select your order and choose the help option. You can also message the restaurant from an active order.

For help from Cal-Eats, email support@calai.info. Include your order reference and the email address on your account.

Account or app help

Open Account → Help & support in the app to send a request and read replies. If you cannot sign in, email caleats-support@calai.info.

For an account-closure request, use the app’s Security & privacy section or contact support. Our team will verify your request before closing your login.

Restaurant partners

Sign in to your business platform to manage orders, your menu and customer messages. Use its support section if you need help from Cal-Eats.
