Help & support

Your account. Your choice.

Delete your Cal-Eats account

You can request deletion of your account and eligible personal data. Cal-Eats is operated by CALAI LTD.

In the app

Start from your account

  1. Open Account → Security & privacy.
  2. Choose Delete your account.
  3. Confirm your password and intention, then send the request. Giving a reason is optional.

You do not need to call or email us to start the in-app request. Your login stays available while our team reviews it.

Without the app

Cannot sign in?

You can request account and personal-data deletion by email. You do not need to reinstall the app.

Email a deletion request

support@calai.info

Send from the email address registered to your Cal-Eats account and say that you want to delete your account and personal data. If you no longer have access to that address, tell us so we can arrange proportionate verification.

Keep your information safe

Do not email passwords, security codes, identity documents, card numbers or bank details. Our team will explain any secure verification needed.

Delete some data and keep your account

You can remove saved addresses in Account. To request deletion of other personal data without closing your account, email support@calai.info from your registered email address. Tell us which information you want removed and that you want to keep your account.

We verify the request and normally act within 30 days, or sooner if the one-calendar-month legal deadline falls earlier. The necessary financial records and unresolved-work exceptions below also apply. We will explain what we can delete and any information we must retain.

What happens next?

Our team checks your identity and request, then removes your login and eligible personal data, revokes account access and confirms completion. This is a deletion request, rather than just disabling your account.

We normally act within 30 days, or sooner if the one-calendar-month legal deadline falls earlier. We will explain any lawful extension, verification need, unresolved order or necessary retained records. Active orders, unresolved order-specific support or payment work, and documented legal holds can delay deletion of the relevant information.

What is deleted, and what may remain?

Account deletion removes your login and eligible personal data, including saved addresses, optional messages, marketing preferences and device notification registrations. Necessary financial and legal records may remain restricted to their permitted purposes; we do not keep every account detail simply because a transaction record is required.

Our regular retention controls also apply these periods to eligible service records:

Order and restaurant chat messages
30 days after each message was created.
Optional allergy notes
30 days after an order completes or is rejected.
Resolved support content and replies
30 days after the case is resolved and last updated.
Order delivery/contact details and instructions
90 days after an order completes or is rejected, including eligible stored print-service copies.
Necessary accounting and transaction evidence
Normally six years from the end of the relevant financial year, or longer where legally required or justified by a documented legal claim.

These periods have exceptions for unresolved work and documented legal holds. Backups, delivered emails, paper receipts and records independently held by restaurants or payment providers have separate controls. Our Privacy Notice explains the details and how retained information is handled.
