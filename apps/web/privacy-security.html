Keep your account protected

A few simple security steps

  • Use a private, unique password. Keep it separate from passwords you use elsewhere. Choose Forgot password on the sign-in screen if you need to reset it.
  • Verify your email. New customer accounts receive a welcome email with a verification link. Verification is needed before your first order. You can request another link from the app.
  • Keep your app and device updated. Use the official app-store download or your existing testing invitation.
  • Keep sensitive details out of messages. Do not send passwords, security codes, full card numbers or bank details to a restaurant or by support email.
  • Tell us if something looks wrong. If you suspect someone has accessed your account, reset your password and contact support@calai.info.

How we protect the service

We use access controls, encrypted connections and protected service credentials. Passwords are stored as hashes, rather than readable text. Selected private messages and notification credentials are encrypted at rest. Authorised service processing can still access information where needed; conversations are not end-to-end encrypted. No service can guarantee absolute security.

You can manage available marketing preferences in your account and location or notification permissions in your phone settings. Optional marketing is separate from essential order and service updates. The Privacy Notice explains these choices in full.

About these public pages

Website cookies and storage

This section covers the Cal-Eats landing page, download page, support page, account-deletion page, Business Applicant Privacy Notice page and this privacy and security page. It does not describe the customer app, signed-in business pages, account-access pages, checkout or another provider’s website.

  • Reviewed public-page scripts do not set cookies or write to local or session storage. These pages do not load advertising or third-party analytics scripts.
  • Download availability comes from our own site. The landing and download pages request the public app-download configuration. They use browser and device information to identify the appropriate store; the download page may redirect to an available official listing.
  • Store links do not include your account or order details. The download script does not copy the page’s query string or verification/reset fragment into an app-store link. Apple and Google handle their own pages under their own notices.
  • Normal connection information is still needed. Your browser sends information such as your network address, requested page and request headers so our hosting service can deliver and protect the website. The Privacy Notice explains operational and security information.

The customer app and signed-in services use storage for functions such as sign-in, basket and address preferences and notification acknowledgements. The Privacy Notice explains that wider scope. This public-page information should not be read as a claim that the entire Cal-Eats service uses no storage.

We are here to help

Questions, concerns or a privacy complaint?

Email support@calai.info and tell us whether your message concerns an order, account security or a privacy request. Include only the information needed to identify your account and explain the issue.

For an order problem, open the order in the app to contact the restaurant or use Help & support for the next steps. Making a privacy request or a genuine complaint does not reduce your consumer rights.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your personal information, you can complain to us and ask us to review our response. You can also contact the Information Commissioner’s Office.

For a data-protection complaint, we will acknowledge receipt within 30 days, investigate and keep you informed of progress and the outcome without undue delay. This acknowledgement period is separate from the deadline for responding to a privacy-rights request, which is normally one calendar month, subject to the applicable verification and lawful-extension rules. It is not a promise that every complaint will be resolved within 30 days.
